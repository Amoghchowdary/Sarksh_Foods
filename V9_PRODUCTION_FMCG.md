# SARKSH Foods V9.0 — Production FMCG Experience

V9.0 replaces the previous artwork-led homepage with a real, responsive customer interface built for a premium packaged-food brand.

## Customer-facing direction

- Deep red / oxblood / maroon brand system with white typography.
- Full-width responsive layout with no exposed side bars or mismatched page background.
- Real product photograph integrated into the same red environment as the surrounding product tiles.
- Clear ordering for home customers and business buyers.
- Consumer-friendly wording across the public pages.
- Consistent public header, sections, cards, forms and footer.
- Existing product, account, order, admin, SEO and Apps Script mechanisms retained.

## Homepage structure

1. Premium red hero with product image and three aligned product tiles.
2. Product proof rail.
3. Product story and pack presentation.
4. Home, food-service and trade buying options.
5. SARKSH Foods brand section.
6. Final order call-to-action.

## Protected mechanisms

The 3D opening animation, official SARKSH Foods logo, customer account system, Google Sheets/Drive backend, admin portal, sitemap/indexing setup and existing API contract are retained.
