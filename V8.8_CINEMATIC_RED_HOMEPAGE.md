# SARKSH Foods V8.8 — Cinematic Red Homepage

This release keeps the cinematic V8.6 landing-page composition and translates it into the requested multi-depth red + white brand system.

## Homepage direction
- Same split editorial/product-photo composition as the approved cinematic reference.
- Deep oxblood, burgundy, maroon and chilli-red layering instead of obsidian/brown.
- All former gold UI typography and lines replaced by white/soft-white.
- Two primary homepage actions remain: **Shop Products** and **Business Orders**.
- Full-bleed product photograph on the right rather than a boxed product card.
- Added the reference-style `TRADITION / IN EVERY / PINCH` image-side message.
- Four compact trust points beneath the calls to action.
- Existing bottom brand rail retained in red/white.

## Preserved production systems
- Official SARKSH Foods logo asset remains unchanged.
- Product photography remains unchanged.
- V8.5 SEO/indexing/canonical/sitemaps/IndexNow logic remains intact.
- Customer portal, admin portal and Apps Script backend remain intact.
- Google Sheets + Drive architecture remains intact.
- 3D intro animation remains intact.
